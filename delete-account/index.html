Delete your CHALO account

Google Play account-deletion resource · Last updated: October 1, 2026

You can permanently delete your CHALO account and associated personal data. This cannot be undone.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open CHALO
  2. Go to Me → Settings → Delete account
  3. Confirm deletion

Your profile is disabled immediately. Residual backups may be purged within 30 days.

Option 2 — Web request

If you cannot open the app, submit the form below. We process verified requests within 30 days.

Or email privacy@chalo.app with your email / CHALO ID.

What we delete

  • Profile (nickname, avatar, bio) and discovery presence
  • Login session / device binding for that account
  • Follows, moments, and in-app social links where tied to the account
  • Wallet balance and VIP status (forfeited; not recoverable)

What we may keep briefly

  • Security / fraud logs and transaction records required by law (limited retention)
  • Content you sent to other users may remain visible to them (e.g. chat copies)

API (developers / automation)

Authenticated in-app deletion: DELETE /v1/auth/account
Public web request: POST /v1/auth/delete-request with JSON { email, numericUid?, reason?, confirm: true }
