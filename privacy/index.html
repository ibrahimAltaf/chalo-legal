Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

CHALO ("we", "us", or "our") operates the CHALO mobile application (Android package com.mycompany.newchalo) and related services (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using CHALO you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use CHALO, we may collect:

  • Account data — nickname, profile photo, bio, email or phone (if you sign in with them), device ID, and account identifiers.
  • Usage & device data — app version, OS, language/locale, approximate country/region, crash logs, and feature usage needed to run rooms, chat, and calls.
  • Communications — private messages, room chat, call signaling metadata (who called whom, duration). Voice/video media in live sessions is processed in real time to deliver the call and is not stored as a permanent recording by default.
  • Social & content — follows, gifts sent, moments you post, family/couple links, reports you submit.
  • Payments & wallet — coin balances, recharge/transaction records, and payout requests. Card numbers are handled by payment processors; we do not store full card details on our servers.
  • Notifications — push notification tokens (e.g. FCM) so we can deliver incoming calls, messages, and system alerts when you allow them.

2. How we use information

  • Provide voice rooms, Meet, chat, gifts, wallet, VIP, and related features.
  • Authenticate you, secure accounts, prevent fraud, spam, and abuse.
  • Send service notifications you enable (including incoming call alerts).
  • Improve reliability, moderation, rankings, and customer support.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

3. Legal bases (where applicable)

We process data to perform our contract with you (providing the Service), for our legitimate interests (security, product improvement, moderation), and where required by law. Where consent is required (e.g. certain notifications or marketing), we ask for it and you may withdraw it in device or in-app settings.

4. Sharing & processors

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Infrastructure & realtime providers needed to run the app (hosting, databases, realtime messaging).
  • Media / RTC providers (e.g. Agora) to deliver live voice and video in rooms and calls.
  • Push & analytics (e.g. Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver notifications and diagnose crashes.
  • Payment processors to complete coin purchases or payouts.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect users' safety and our rights.

Other users may see your public profile, room presence, gifts, and messages you send them according to how you use the Service.

5. Data retention

We keep account and transaction data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for security, disputes, and legal obligations. You may request deletion (see below). Backups may persist for a limited time after deletion.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and session tokens. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please protect your device and login details.

7. Children

CHALO is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Your rights & choices

  • Update profile information in the app (Me → Edit profile).
  • Control notification and microphone/camera permissions in device settings.
  • Use in-app privacy controls where available (Settings → Privacy).
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your account via in-app Delete account or by emailing privacy@chalo.app.

Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR/CCPA), you may have additional rights. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.
